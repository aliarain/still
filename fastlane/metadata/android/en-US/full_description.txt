Still is a minimalist launcher for Android that gives you a clean, text-first home screen. Keep the apps you use most within reach, and find everything else in a fast, searchable app drawer. Make your phone feel simpler and use it with more intention.

Set limits that actually hold
Give any app a daily time budget. When it is spent, opening that app shows a calm block screen instead of the app, and budgets reset each day. Define recurring focus windows, such as overnight or work hours, where chosen apps are paused entirely. Focus windows can cross midnight and repeat on the weekdays you pick.

Find apps fast
Search installed apps by name or browse with A-Z fast scrolling. Search ranks matches by how often you actually open each app, so the app you mean comes first. Move or hide the search bar, and optionally open the only matching app automatically. Rename apps, reorder favorites, and access app info or uninstall apps directly from the launcher.

Focus on what matters
Choose a few favorite apps for your home screen, or leave it empty. Group apps into folders and hide the ones you do not want to see every time you open your phone. A home screen widget shows today's screen time, your active focus window, and the budgets closest to running out.

Make it yours
Use app names alone or show icons. Adjust fonts, text size, spacing, alignment, and icon placement. Choose light, dark, black, system, or dynamic colors. Keep your wallpaper, dim it for readability, or match it to your theme.

Keep the essentials close
Add an optional clock and date, battery level, notification dots, or web shortcuts. Assign apps to left and right swipe gestures, double-tap to lock your screen, and choose a portrait or landscape layout.

Back up and move on
Export your favorites, folders, hidden apps, focus limits, schedules and preferences to a single file, and restore it on another device.

Private and open source
Still does not track you or collect personal information. Its source code is available to review and contribute to.

For a distraction-free home screen, real app limits, and a more focused Android experience, make Still your launcher.

Permissions:
Usage access is used to read per-app foreground time so daily limits can be enforced, screen time can be shown, and search can be ranked by real usage. Nothing leaves your device. The app uses the Accessibility permission for the optional "Double tap to lock" feature. Notification access is used only to draw notification dots on app entries. No personal data is collected by the app.
